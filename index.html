About me

Operating envelopes as a common language between network operators and grid users.

I am a researcher at the Smart Grids Lab of the University of Liège, where I am starting a PhD under the supervision of Prof. Damien Ernst. My work sits between optimisation, regulatory analysis and distribution network management.

An operating envelope tells a grid user, period by period, how much power it may inject or draw without endangering the network. I study how these limits should be computed, how they should be allocated between users when the network is constrained, and how users could trade them among themselves.

I hold an MSc in Energy Engineering from the University of Liège. My master's thesis dealt with the coordination of transmission and distribution system operators through power operating envelopes, computed with a QC relaxation of the AC power flow equations on networks that include phase-shifting transformers. It was carried out with the industrial partners RTE and Gérédis and with TU Delft, and received the Elia Group Award and the AIM Award for the best master's thesis.

Alongside this, I contributed to work on Remote Renewable Energy Hubs presented at ECOS 2026, and I take part in teaching the Energy Market course at the University of Liège. I work in English and French.

Research

Three questions about the limits that networks place on their users.

Optimisation

Computing envelopes

Envelopes that remain safe under the AC power flow equations, obtained from convex relaxations such as the QC relaxation, including networks with phase-shifting transformers. With RTE, Gérédis and TU Delft.

Regulation

From contract to operation

How a long-term flexible connection contract becomes a limit applied close to real time, and how curtailment is shared between users of a constrained network. Includes analysis of European and Walloon law.

Market design

Markets for envelope rights

Secondary markets in which grid users exchange the capacity their envelopes grant them, so that scarce network room goes to those who value it most.
